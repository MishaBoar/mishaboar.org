**Last researched:** 7 October 2026

Some have asked which charities accept **Dogecoin** and provide or support civilian relief in Gaza. This page offers a shortlist, with links to their official donation pages and reports.

This page is my attempt to keep things practical and boring in the good way: use official charity pages, choose the kind of relief you want to support, confirm where your donation will go, and keep the receipt.

{{< admonition warning "A Gaza response is not a Gaza-only donation" >}}
**I have not verified a Gaza-only DOGE donation route for any of these six organizations.** They report relief work in Gaza, but their general crypto donation pages do not necessarily reserve your gift for Gaza. These are not direct transfers to families, either. If Gaza-only use is essential to you, obtain confirmation from the charity **before sending**. A note saying "for Gaza" is not enough unless the charity accepts that restriction.
{{< /admonition >}}

Its [supported-currency list](https://thegivingblock.com/resources/cryptocurrency-we-accept/) includes Dogecoin and says listed currencies are accepted by every nonprofit in its network. Still, select **Dogecoin (DOGE)** in the live checkout before sending.

### Direct Relief

Direct Relief supplies medicines and medical equipment to health providers. In **May 2026**, it reported delivering **nearly 16 tons** of medicines and supplies to Gaza, supporting International Medical Corps hospitals and Project HOPE health facilities. [Delivery report](https://www.directrelief.org/2026/05/direct-relief-gaza-medical-aid-delivery/).

**Donate DOGE:** [Direct Relief on The Giving Block](https://thegivingblock.com/donate/direct-relief/).

**Gaza designation:** When checked, its crypto form's designation menu did not include Gaza. Contact Direct Relief first if your gift must be Gaza-only; do not assume choosing "Where Needed Most" achieves that.

[Financial reports and donation policies](https://www.directrelief.org/about/finance/).

### International Medical Corps

International Medical Corps provides emergency, surgical, maternal, and other health services in Gaza. Its response page, checked in **October 2026**, reports **three field hospitals and two multipurpose health centers**, with more than 400 beds across its operations. [Gaza response](https://internationalmedicalcorps.org/emergency-response/israel-gaza-crisis/).

**Donate DOGE:** [Official crypto page](https://internationalmedicalcorps.org/get-involved/other-ways-to-give/donate-cryptocurrency/) or [International Medical Corps on The Giving Block](https://thegivingblock.com/donate/international-medical-corps/).

**Gaza designation:** Its crypto page says gifts are converted to USD immediately and used where needed most. Ask the charity whether it can accept a Gaza-restricted DOGE gift before using that form.

[Financial reports](https://internationalmedicalcorps.org/who-we-are/accountability-financials/financial-reports/).

### Project HOPE

Project HOPE provides primary care, maternal health services, nutrition support, and clean water. Its **August 2026** report describes work through **seven primary health clinics and one mobile clinic**, with about **36,000 outpatient consultations** that month. Consultations are visits, not necessarily different patients. [August situation report](https://www.projecthope.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Project-HOPE-Gaza-August-2026-SitRep.pdf).

**Donate DOGE:** [Project HOPE on The Giving Block](https://thegivingblock.com/donate/project-hope/).

[Financial reports](https://www.projecthope.org/about/financials/).

### World Central Kitchen

World Central Kitchen focuses on emergency meals, working through field and community kitchens. In its **20 May 2026** update, it reported **more than 360 million meals** served in Gaza since the conflict began, while warning that funding pressure was reducing daily output. [Gaza operations update](https://wck.org/news/gaza-questions/).

**Donate DOGE:** [World Central Kitchen on The Giving Block](https://thegivingblock.com/donate/world-central-kitchen/).

**Gaza designation:** The [appeal headed "Help us serve nourishing meals in Gaza"](https://donate.wck.org/campaign/749399/donate) describes support for the broader Middle East response and links to a general crypto page. Confirm Gaza-only treatment with WCK if that matters to you.

[Financial reports](https://wck.org/resources/).

### World Food Program USA

World Food Program USA is a **U.S. nonprofit supporting the UN World Food Programme (WFP)**, not the UN agency itself. WFP's Palestine emergency page, checked in **October 2026**, reports reaching around **1.5 million people each month in Gaza**, through food, nutrition services, and cash assistance. [WFP's Gaza response](https://www.wfp.org/emergencies/palestine-emergency).

**Donate DOGE:** [Official WFP USA crypto page](https://wfpusa.org/give/crypto/), which explicitly lists Dogecoin. This is a general giving page; ask WFP USA about earmarking a gift for Gaza.

[WFP USA's audited FY2025 financial statements](https://wfpusa.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/WFPUSA-Financial-Report-2025.pdf).

### Action Against Hunger USA

The Action Against Hunger network works on hunger, malnutrition, and water access in Gaza and the West Bank. Its Gaza response includes meals and food supplies, clean water, hygiene support, and nutrition services for children and pregnant women. [Gaza and West Bank response](https://www.actionagainsthunger.org/location/middle-east/west-bank-gaza/).

**Donate DOGE:** Use the U.S. organization's [official crypto page](https://www.actionagainsthunger.org/take-action/make-a-gift/cryptocurrency-donations/) or [The Giving Block profile](https://thegivingblock.com/donate/action-against-hunger/). Confirm whether a requested restriction covers Gaza alone or Gaza and the West Bank.

[Financial reports](https://www.actionagainsthunger.org/who-we-are/financials-reports/).

## Fees and how much reaches the relief effort

There is no reliable public figure for how much of a particular DOGE gift will be spent in Gaza. The Giving Block charges subscription and transaction fees, but its [pricing page](https://thegivingblock.com/packages/) does not give one rate that can be applied to every charity here. Ask the charity about processing and conversion costs if these affect your choice.

Program-spending percentages are historical, organization-wide measures. They can include donated goods as well as cash, and they cover costs such as staff, transport, and clinic operations, not just supplies handed to families. The financial reports linked above are useful for accountability, but **they cannot tell us that $100 in DOGE produces a particular dollar amount of Gaza relief**.

## Choosing where to give

Choose based on the need you want to support: medicines and health services, meals, or broader hunger and nutrition relief. No organization can eliminate access, security, or diversion risks. Nor does the type of aid alone establish which option is safest: [research on humanitarian diversion](https://www.calpnetwork.org/publication/perception-vs-reality-diversion-risks-in-humanitarian-assistance/) cautions against assuming cash is inherently riskier than goods.

## Before you donate

- Start from the charity's **official website** or its linked The Giving Block profile.
- Select **Dogecoin (DOGE)** and use the address and network instructions shown in that checkout, not an address copied from social media.
- If your gift must be Gaza-only, confirm that restriction **before** transferring it. If the charity cannot accept it, choose another donation route.
- Keep the receipt and any written confirmation of the designation.
- For a large gift, ask about fees, restrictions, and reporting in advance.
